Questions

Which banks can my viewers use?

Any bank app that scans KHQR, including ABA and other Bakong-connected apps.

How are payments matched?

Your phone forwards bank notification text to a private webhook. LiveTip KH matches its amount and currency to a recent pending tip. This is not a direct bank API or independent proof of settlement, so creators should verify their bank records before relying on a match.

Does it work with OBS?

Yes. Every overlay is a link you add as a Browser Source. Other apps that support browser sources work too.

Can alerts be read in Khmer?

Yes. Donor messages can be read aloud in Khmer, and viewers can send their own recordings.

Where do I get help?

Message us on Telegram. Setup guides are available after you sign in.
